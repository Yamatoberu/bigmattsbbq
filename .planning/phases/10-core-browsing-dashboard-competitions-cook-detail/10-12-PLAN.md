  <what-built>
Two gap-closure plans landed on top of the shipped Phase 10 browsing surface:

- **G-10-1 (discoverability):** a new `/sca/cooks` index lists every recorded cook newest-first with its date, total score, and placement, each row linking to that cook's detail page. The SCA nav bar now reads Dashboard · Competitions · Cooks. This supersedes the original D-11 decision that cook detail stay drill-down-only.
- **G-10-2 (comparison scope):** `buildComparisonTable` now takes an optional `aggregateSource` and `aggregateScopeLabel`. Competition detail passes every recorded cook as the aggregate source, so Worst Cook / Best Cook / Cook Averages are all-time figures labelled `(All Time)` and a sentence above the table states that scope. The Dashboard's call site is unchanged — its columns keep the plain DASH-02 labels and event-wide values.

Automated coverage: new unit tests for `sortCooksByRecencyDesc` and for the aggregate-source behavior, including a regression case asserting a single-cook table's average cell differs from that cook's own cell. Page rendering still has no automated coverage, which is why this pass exists.
  </what-built>
