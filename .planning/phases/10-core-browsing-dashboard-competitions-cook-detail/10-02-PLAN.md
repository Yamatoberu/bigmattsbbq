<objective>
Build the two pure-logic modules behind the Dashboard's summary cards (DASH-01) and its data-driven "what stands out" section (DASH-03).

Purpose: These are the only two places in the phase where numbers get reduced. Centralizing them here keeps the Dashboard and Competition detail pages from computing "best cook" two different ways, and makes the real-data edge cases (competition 4 has one cook with NO score row at all) testable without a browser — the repo has no React rendering test infrastructure, so pure functions are the only automatable surface.
Output: `lib/sca/aggregates.ts`, `lib/sca/insights.ts`, and their two test files.
</objective>
