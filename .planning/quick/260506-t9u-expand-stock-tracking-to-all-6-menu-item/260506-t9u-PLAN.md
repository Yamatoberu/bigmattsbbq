<objective>
Expand per-item capacity tracking from 2 items (pulled pork, brisket) to all 6 items (the 2 originals + sauce + the 3 packages: family night, backyard host, freezer filler). Each item gets its own capacity and reserved columns at both the drop level and the per-location pickup level, gated atomically by the existing reserve/release RPCs, and surfaced through DropDTO.soldOut so the storefront can render accurate sold-out states.

Purpose: The storefront currently only enforces capacity for pulled pork and brisket. Sauce has no capacity tracking; packages rely on Square's `bundleVariationId` inventory which is decoupled from the Supabase drop. The owner needs each of the 6 SKU surfaces (2 individual meats + 1 sauce + 3 packages) to have an independently-set capacity that the UI honors.

Output: A new migration, expanded DTO + drop server logic, expanded checkout reservation, and OrderLanding wired to the new soldOut flags. Existing drops test seeds updated so the suite continues to pass.
</objective>
